The second brain: July-2026 research, and the build plan

Follow-up to the memory audit (verdict: accumulating, not learning). Live survey of the July-2026 memory landscape, scored against the audit's seven disqualifiers, then scoped into a standalone brain that rawgentic hooks into.

Recommendation

Native-first working memory + a standalone, self-hosted, typed second brain — storage engine chosen by a bake-off on our own data. Nothing off-the-shelf ships retrieval telemetry (the audit's #1 gap), so it's custom work under every option. Curation — not storage — is what separates systems, and the strongest curation in the market is Claude Code's own auto-memory. The engine underneath is a commodity; the bake-off picks it with evidence instead of a bet.
